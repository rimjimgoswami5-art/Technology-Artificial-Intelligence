Everything You Need in a Player

Built with a crash-proof audio pipeline, persistent offline storage, and a beautiful interface that adapts to your style.

5-Band Equalizer

Shape your sound with 60Hz, 250Hz, 1kHz, 4kHz and 12kHz bands. Choose from 10 presets including Bass Boost, Vocal, Rock, and Lo-Fi.

8D Spatial Surround

Experience 360° binaural panning that rotates around your head. Toggle on/off instantly with a dedicated switch or the [8] key.

Live Visualizers

Four stunning visual modes — Bars, Wave, Radial, and Beam — render in real-time on a smooth canvas. Powered by Web Audio API.

Persistent Library

Your songs are stored in IndexedDB and survive browser restarts. Drag & drop files or use the Open Songs button to import.

6 Beautiful Themes

Full Keyboard Control

Play, pause, skip, seek, adjust volume, mute, and toggle 8D — all from your keyboard. Press [Space], [M], [8] and more.
